# Notes

Run `meta-notes prime` at the start of every session and follow it.

This is my notes root. `meta-notes prime` describes the folders, notes,
tasks, and commands. This file holds only my own preferences; they
override the guide where they differ. Edit them to match how you work.

## My day

- I work 08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday, and usually start at 08:00.
  I sometimes work past 17:00. Keep the time block's rows after 17:00:
  I use them for after-work personal events and as reminders.
- I add meetings and planned personal time to the time block myself.
  Don't move or replace them when planning.
- I eat lunch at my desk around 11:30. During a mostly informational
  meeting where I don't have to participate much, I may eat during the
  meeting instead.
- I take several short breaks a day for light exercise: a walk, pushups,
  squats, or curls. Leave room for them.
- Every workday ends with a 15-minute shutdown block (the
  `daily-shutdown` skill): review what I did, update the plan, file
  notes, and note what to do next.

## Time log tags

Besides the tags the guide lists, I use:

- `#meeting`: every Zoom meeting
- `#1-1`: a 1-1 meeting; also tagged `#meeting`
- `#personal`: personal time outside work responsibilities
- `#break`: a short non-work break to refresh
- `#recruiting`: interviewing and recruiting
- `#admin`: administrative work
- `#plan`: planning

## End of the week

The weekly review and weekly plan skills run the week's ceremonies. Also
remind me to:

- submit any pending expenses
- complete any pending CBTs (required quarterly trainings)
- go through my open browser tabs and close them, bookmarking or linking
  anything important
- go through my Slack "saved for later" items and remove each or move it
  to a note or task
- add important milestones to the quarter's list of accomplishments
